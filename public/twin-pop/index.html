TwinPop is a quick, colorful matching game. Tap to add shapes, then drag two identical shapes together to pop a match and score points. Keep matching to build a combo.

Each shape has a timer, so look sharp and make your matches before time runs out. Start with a few familiar shapes, then try a trickier level with more variety and a faster pace.

🎮 Quick rounds · 🫧 Colorful shapes · 🔥 Combo scoring

How to play

1. Spawn a shape

Tap an open spot in the play area to add a new colorful shape.

2. Find its twin

Look for another shape with the same color and form.

3. Drag to match

Move the pair together to pop them, earn points, and keep your run alive.
