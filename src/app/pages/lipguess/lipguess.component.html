💋 Watch · Recognize · Guess

Watch the lips. Guess the word.

LipGuess is a fun visual word-guessing game that challenges you to recognize words by watching lip movements.

Look closely at the mouth, study the lip shape, and choose the correct answer. Each round helps you become more familiar with different sounds, words, and visual speech patterns.
